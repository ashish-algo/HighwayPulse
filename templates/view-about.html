HighwayPulse

Highway Defect Prioritization & Maintenance Planning Dashboard

1. Project Purpose & Problem Statement

Highways form the backbone of national transit, yet road authorities face thousands of concurrent defects—from minor potholes to critical guardrail failures. Maintenance funds and field crews are finite. HighwayPulse is an engineering dashboard developed to address this challenge: it systematically evaluates defects against multiple operational criteria to generate a transparent, explainable priority score that assists maintenance teams in allocating resources effectively.

2. Core Architectural Pillars

  • Zero Framework Overhead: Built strictly with semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, and modular Vanilla JavaScript. Runs locally by opening index.html directly.
  • Multi-Criteria Scoring Engine: Deterministic 4-factor heuristic combining Severity (40%), Traffic Exposure (25%), Safety Risk (25%), and Dwell Time (10%).
  • Geographic Defect Tracking: Leaflet.js interactive map plotting corridor coordinates across India with priority color coding.
  • Maintenance Work Order Planning: Workload scheduling, milestone tracking, and crew conflict detection.
  • Resilient Offline Fallback: If Leaflet or Chart.js CDN assets are unavailable, the application gracefully renders tabular and pure-CSS visual fallbacks without crashing.

3. Prioritization Formula & Heuristic Math

Priority Score = (Severity × 0.40) + (Traffic Exposure × 0.25) + (Safety Risk × 0.25) + (Time Unresolved × 0.10)

Classifications: Critical (80–100), High (60–79), Medium (35–59), Low (0–34).
